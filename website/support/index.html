Support

Get the shield running and troubleshoot filtering, schedules, and locked settings.

Install or update

Follow the installation steps for your browser. After installing or updating, reload any social feeds that were already open.

A post slipped through

Open No Spoil 6 from the toolbar. Check that the shield is active and the site is enabled under Settings → Filters. Everything about GTA 6 covers ordinary news and trailers as well as story spoilers. Add a distinctive word or phrase under Block phrases if the same kind of post keeps slipping through.

The shield checks page text and metadata such as video titles and image descriptions. Images, audio, or video with no matching metadata may stay visible.

A post or page was covered by mistake

Use the eye button to reveal a single post. In Settings → Filters, you can add an Allow phrase or turn filtering off for a platform. If an entire page is covered, look for a matching rule in Site blocks.

Protection starts or ends at the wrong time

Schedules use the device’s local clock. Check your timezone, start date, and daily windows in the toolbar popup. A window like 22:00–02:00 continues into the following day.

The shield is locked

Open Settings → Shield lock to check the end time. If you need to change your settings sooner, start early unlock, wait 30 minutes, and type the sentence shown in the extension.

Report a problem

Use the Support section of the No Spoil 6 Chrome Web Store listing. Include your browser, the extension version, the affected website, and the steps to reproduce the problem. Describe the issue without sharing spoiler text or private information.
