Privacy policy

No Spoil 6 filters GTA 6 references in your browser. The extension processes page content on your device and does not send it to the developer, third parties, or a filtering server.

Website access

The extension requests access to HTTP and HTTPS pages so the shield can run across social feeds and other websites. While the shield is active, it checks page text, titles, image descriptions, and links to decide which posts to cover. It does not save the pages you browse or keep a browsing history.

Preferences

Your mode, schedules, site blocks, and custom phrases are saved in the browser’s local extension storage. You can export these preferences to a file and import them into another browser.

Optional browser sync

Browser sync is off by default. If you turn it on, the extension stores your filter preferences, schedules, phrases, and site block rules in your browser’s sync service. Your browser provider manages that data. The extension excludes page content, activity counts, and lock state from sync and settings files.

Protection activity

With activity enabled, the extension keeps daily counts by platform for covers, page blocks, and reveals. Counts are stored on this device and shown for the most recent 30 days. They contain no page titles, post text, or URLs. You can clear activity or turn it off in Settings.

Accounts, tracking, and sharing

The extension requires no No Spoil 6 account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics. It does not sell or transmit the page content it checks. Except when you enable browser sync, settings stay on your device or in files you export.

This website

The website serves the interactive preview, extension downloads, and documentation. The preview uses sample content and does not read your browser activity. The site code sets no cookies and contains no analytics scripts. The hosting provider may keep standard web request logs.
